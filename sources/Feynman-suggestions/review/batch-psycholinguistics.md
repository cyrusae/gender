## Deep-dive entries (Elpers 2022 section of `paper-deep-dives.md`)

- VERIFIED: N = 375; Exp. 1 null; Exp. 2 replicated; "Stage-1 protocol on OSF" (the OSF data link in the PDF is osf.io/3fnhm, a different project from the protocol DOI in the deep-dive, osf.io/9c2ak; not checked which is correct).
- VERIFIED: Exp. 1 "registered" mixed-effects and Bayesian analyses; the paper says P&B's original did not report descriptive statistics for Exp. 1.
- UNCHECKED: "Exp. 2 ~n = 150." The paper says 189 were recruited; the analysed n (after exclusions) was not read.
- UNCHECKED: "M = 3.71 (SD 2.04) vs 2.56 (SD 1.21)", χ²(1) = 11.80, d = 0.66, BF10 = 5.01 × 10^10. Figure 1 is consistent with ~3.7 vs ~2.6, but the exact statistics are on PDF page 7, which was not read.
- Note: the deep-dive says Exp. 1 "registered Bayesian analyses overwhelmingly supported the null." Consistent with the paper's BF10 = 0.021 (by participants), but the paper's text is more conditional (it shows the null holds only when inter-participant and inter-item variance is modelled; the t-tests gave p = .001 for the by-participant analysis, which is what "modelling variance" removes).

## Notes for the reading list (suggested edits, not made)

1. **Fix the wrong open-access link** for the 2003 chapter (§1 row 2). escholarship 0jt9w8zf is the 2000 CogSci paper. Find the chapter's own link or use the MIT Press DOI.
2. **Correct the Boroditsky & Schmidt 2000 description** (§1 row 3). The paper has no adjective-generation task. Its studies are animal/artifact gender intuitions (Exp. 1) and object-name memory with proper names (Exp. 2).
3. **Update Xiao et al.** from "preprint, venue unverified" to *Cognitive Science* 50:e70176 (2026), DOI 10.1111/cogs.70176. Keep the person-noun control claim flagged as unverified.
4. **Mahajan & Durgin**: change "implicit similarity task" to "implicit potency-rating task (Konishi-style scales)," and note that only Exp. 1 was in English.
5. **Kurinski & Sera**: change "gender-stereotyped properties" to "gender attribution to objects in a longitudinal classroom study." Keep the "human analog" claim as a judgment, not a finding.
6. **Mickan et al.**: quote the abstract's "non-documented aspect of the experimental procedure" rather than "undocumented stimulus details."
7. **Samuel et al. 2019**: the 32%/43% figures match what Elpers 2022 quotes, but the primary text was not read. Read the results section before using the numbers in the write-up.
8. **Phillips & Boroditsky**: "never peer-reviewed in a journal" is accurate, but the proceedings are peer reviewed. Use "never published in a journal."
9. **OSF ics7w**: open in a browser, confirm the description, then keep or drop the "preregistration" label.
