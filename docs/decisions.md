| 2026-10-06 | **Pre-registered Phase 2 reading** (before any Phase 2 result). Spanish; inner layers; items = nouns known by all three Qwen sizes (shared set). **Primary direction**: difference of class means (fem − masc) on the ending-matched set (no hyperparameters); secondary: logistic probe on the same set; comparison: same two trained on regular nouns, raw and after the Phase 1 verb and rank-2 erasers. Score s(x) = projection on the direction. **Gender index** p(x) = (s(x) − mean s(regular test masc)) / (mean s(regular test fem) − mean s(regular test masc)): 0 = like a typical masculine noun, 1 = like a typical feminine one. 95% CIs by bootstrap over items. **Exceptions (primary test)**: exception AUC = do feminine exceptions (*mano*, *foto*, *moto*, *libido*, *polio*) score above masculine ones (*problema*, *día*, *mapa*…)? Bias-free; a spelling detector gives AUC < 0.5. Outcome rows (design doc): *follows gender* = AUC CI lower bound > 0.5 in a majority of inner layers; *follows spelling* = CI upper bound < 0.5 in a majority; *-ma/clippings right, día/mano wrong* = judged from per-subgroup mean p; otherwise *nothing sorts cleanly*. **Homographs**: AUC (f vs m homographs) in the noun frame (*mi X*) vs the verb frame (*yo X* / *usted X*); gender beyond spelling = AUC_noun − AUC_verb > 0 with CI excluding 0 (a pure spelling detector scores both frames equally, since the endings match). **mar-type**: mean p(*la X*) − p(*el X*) > 0 = gender read from the article; split by each model's lean (with vs against). **Regular held-out**: AUC of the matched-set direction on regular test nouns (does an ending-free direction also sort ordinary nouns?) | decide what counts before seeing results | — |
